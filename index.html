Combat abilities

2 points at creation, 1 per level, cap 10 per ability.

Step 5

Civil abilities

1 point at creation, then one at levels 2, 6, 10, 14, 18 and 22. Cap 5. Green numbers are racial bonuses.

Step 6

Talents

Talent points at levels 1, 3, 8, 13 and 18. Racial talents are free and shown in Step 1.

Step 7

Skills & spells

To show the real skill and item icons, click Import game icons and select both Icons.pak and Patch1.pak from Divinity Original Sin 2\DefEd\Data in your Steam library. The page cuts the icons out in your browser and keeps them in this browser only; nothing is uploaded. On a phone, import rivellon-icons.json instead: a small icon pack made from those same files.

Memory

Locked skills need more points in their school. Source skills cost Source Points and extra memory. Racial skills are free and always memorised.

Step 8

Equipment

Click a slot in your loadout, filter the list, then click an item to equip it. Unique items carry their real bonuses from the game files; for regular gear, set the rarity and add the bonuses your copy rolled.

Hover (or tap) anything for its full details. Skills and unique items are read from the stat files in your own DOS2 install: names, AP, Source cost, cooldown, memory, requirements, descriptions and item bonuses. Damage is shown as a percentage of base damage because the real number depends on level and gear. Item attribute bonuses are the base values; the game raises them a little with item level. Talents come from the wikis because they aren't in the stat files. Icons are original glyphs unless you import your own from the game files.
